constellations.json trims both source files to what this layer draws: each
constellation's IAU 3-letter id, English name, RA/Dec centroid (deg), and
its stick-figure line segments (RA/Dec deg, same convention as
stars-hyg41-m5.bin: RA in degrees 0-360, not hours).
